# VN30 point-in-time snapshots and HOSE prices

`scripts/build_market_extensions.py` regenerates the membership table, per-snapshot adjusted price, volume and approximate trading-value panels, and `coverage_by_snapshot.csv`. Each snapshot has 30 named constituents, a dated exchange notice URL (some original HOSE PDFs are hosted by brokers), announcement date, and a bounded effective interval. These are the **stocks in the index at that time**, including former constituents; current VN30 membership is never projected backwards.

Verified snapshots: both half-yearly reviews in 2015, 2016, 2017 and 2018, July 2019, July 2025 and July 2026 (11 snapshots). The January 2019 review, the 2020–mid-2025 reviews and the January 2026 review remain **uncovered**. July 2026 is conservatively stopped at 2026-09-30 pending verification of later 2026 reviews. The first January 2015 sessions need the previous review's constituent list. No backtest should bridge these gaps or treat a snapshot as valid past its documented end.

Values are inherited unchanged from `data_hose`: VCI adjusted closes in VND, matched volumes, and *approximated* trading value (`adjusted close × volume`), not reported turnover. `coverage_by_snapshot.csv` counts actual nonblank cells. The script asserts 30 distinct members per review, checks that each is present in the HOSE listing master on the effective date, and rejects missing price/volume/value columns. It does not fill suspended or missing days.

Run `python scripts/build_market_extensions.py` from any directory. New snapshots should be added only after verifying all 30 constituents and the announcement/effective dates against a dated notice. An index reweighting alone does not change constituents, but it may change index weights; this panel contains member stocks, not historical VN30 index weights. Do not publish an all-years panel until all review intervals are covered and delisted-price availability is audited.
